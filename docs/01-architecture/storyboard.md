### Scenario console interaction
- Scenario chips switch the recording. Switching mid-run is guarded: a hold of about 0.6 s arms the change, a ring fills while held, and releasing early cancels it.
- Hold-to-arm also applies to "tamper with a copy". The edit is made to a COPY of the ledger and re-verified; the original is never changed. The result lines say so.
- Cooldown: after a switch or a tamper, the controls are disabled for about 2 s so the event log stays readable.
- Every action writes one line to a timestamped event log (simulated clock time plus a wall-clock stamp), in neutral wording, e.g. "Copy edited at entry 150; chain broken at entry 150 (bad_signature)". The log never says "attack succeeded" or "caught the attacker".
- Approve, Override and Reject are disabled in replay and show "Not available in replay". The backend has no authentication yet, so live approval is a later phase.
- Shortcuts: `1` to `5`, `Space`, `R`, `T`, `A`, `P`, `Q`, `F`, `G`, `C`, and `?` for the list.
